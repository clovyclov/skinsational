Dermal Filler FAQs

Everything you need to know about our physician-supervised facial filler treatments in Wyandotte, MI.

Most hyaluronic acid dermal fillers last anywhere from 9 to 18 months, depending on the treatment area, the specific filler formulation used, and your personal metabolic rate. High-mobility areas like the lips typically metabolize faster (around 9-12 months), while cheek and jawline contouring can easily last 15-24 months.

Client comfort is our top priority. We apply a medical-grade topical numbing cream prior to your treatment. Additionally, our premium FDA-approved fillers are formulated with lidocaine, ensuring continuous numbing and minimal discomfort throughout the injection process.

Botox is a neurotoxin that relaxes underlying muscles to soften dynamic expression wrinkles (like forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet). Dermal fillers are gel-like hyaluronic acid substances designed to physically restore lost volume, hydrate skin, and sculpt contours (like lips, cheeks, and jawlines). Often, both treatments complement each other for complete facial rejuvenation.
